## Design Context

### Users
Visitors to Moghal Saif's personal website: collaborators, readers, recruiters, and people evaluating his work, writing, and taste.

### Brand Personality
Warm, focused, and thoughtful. The site should feel personal and intentional, but more professional than playful.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, seamless personal portfolio with a clean sans-serif type system, soft white text, dock-like controls, full-screen sections, and smooth Framer Motion transitions. Avoid page-like borders and heavy framed layouts; the experience should feel like one continuous black canvas.

### Design Principles
- Lead with clarity: projects and writing should be easy to scan before they are opened.
- Keep the tone personal, but present it with professional spacing and hierarchy.
- Use Framer Motion for purposeful reveals, carousels, and hover feedback.
- Prefer simple lists, metadata, and thoughtful summaries over decorative metaphors.
- Make every section responsive across phone, tablet, desktop, and wide displays.
